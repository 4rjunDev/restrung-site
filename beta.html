Testing Restrung

Thanks for trying the app before it launches. Restrung is a real ordering app for racket stringing at Kingston Racquet Centre, so the orders you place are real and will be strung. The one thing that is not real yet is payment.

Getting the app

Install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the invitation link we sent you. TestFlight installs Restrung and updates it whenever we ship a new build.

Paying during the beta

Card payments run in test mode. When the payment sheet appears, use the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 with any future expiry date and any 3-digit code. Nothing is charged to anyone. Settle up for the stringing at the counter as usual.

Please do not enter a real card. It would be declined, and nothing would be saved.

What to try

Create an account, order a restring, pay with the test card, then follow the order on its tracking screen. Before the stringer starts, try changing a racket's tension from the tracking screen. Save a racket so the next order takes a few taps. If you have questions about an order, the shop's details are on the order itself.

Telling us what you found

In TestFlight, open Restrung and use Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in the app and choose the feedback option that appears. Email racketsrestrung@gmail.com works too. Anything confusing is worth telling us, not only things that break.

Good to know

Beta builds expire 90 days after they are published; TestFlight will offer the next build before then. Your account and orders carry over to the App Store version when it launches.
