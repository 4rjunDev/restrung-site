These terms apply when you use the Restrung app or our stringing service, operated by AD Studios in Kingston, Ontario, Canada. By placing an order you agree to them.

The service

We restring tennis rackets to the string and tension you choose in the app. Standard turnaround is about 2 to 3 days from drop-off; rush orders are ready the next day. Turnaround times are estimates, not guarantees. Pickup and drop-off at your address are optional extras and depend on availability.

Prices and payment

The total shown when you place an order is the price you pay. It includes sales tax (HST in Ontario), which is shown as its own line. You pay by card or Apple Pay in the app when you place the order. Card payments are processed by Stripe. Rackets are released at pickup once payment has been received. Prices may change for future orders.

Cancellations

An order cannot be cancelled by you once it is placed, because a refund still costs the stringer card processing fees. If you need to change or cancel an order, contact the shop you ordered from (its details are on your order): the shop can cancel it before stringing starts, in which case your card is refunded in full, usually within 5 to 10 business days. If stringing has begun, work done so far may be charged and any refund is decided by the shop case by case. Once a racket is strung the full price is due.

Your racket

You are responsible for telling us the correct string and tension. Stringing at high tension or with worn frames carries a risk of frame damage that we cannot fully control. We take reasonable care with every racket in our possession. If a racket is damaged through our fault, our liability is limited to repair or the racket's reasonable replacement value. We are not liable for indirect losses.

Rackets not collected within 60 days of being ready may be treated as abandoned after we have tried to contact you.

Restrung Club

Club plans are monthly memberships that cover stringing labor for a set number of restrings, or unlimited restrings, as described in the app. Each membership is paid one month at a time and covers labor until the end of the paid month. Plan prices are before sales tax; HST is added to each charge. If you join by card, your first month is charged when you join and your card is charged the same amount automatically each month until you cancel; if a renewal charge fails, labor is not covered until a charge succeeds; we retry for 3 days, then the membership ends. If you pay by e-Transfer, the membership starts once we receive your first payment, and you send each following month before the current one ends; if a month is not paid, coverage ends automatically. Changing to a different plan takes effect once we approve it. You can cancel from the app at any time; cancellation stops future charges and your coverage continues to the end of the month you have paid for. Paid months are not refunded, and unused restrings do not carry over.

For stringers

If you sign up to string through Restrung, your shop appears to customers once we approve it and you have connected a Stripe account. Customers pay you through Stripe: the payment is made on your Stripe account, Stripe deducts its card processing fee (2.9% + 30¢ per payment for Canadian cards; international cards cost 0.8% more, and Stripe's current rates apply), and Restrung's fee is passed to Restrung at the same time. Stripe pays the rest out to your bank on your payout schedule. Your Stripe account is governed by Stripe's own agreement, which you accept when you connect it.

What Restrung charges. On pay as you go, 6% of each card order covers both Stripe's processing fee and Restrung, and is never less than Stripe's fee on that order. On a monthly plan you pay the plan price instead of a Restrung fee per order; Stripe's processing fee still applies to each payment, and rackets beyond your plan's monthly limit are charged at the pay-as-you-go rate. Plans and rates are shown in the app before you choose, and we will give notice in the app before changing them.

Cancellations and refunds. Customers cannot cancel an order once it is placed; only you can, from your queue in the app. When you cancel before starting stringing, the customer is refunded in full and Restrung's fee on that order is returned to you. Stripe does not return its own processing fee on a refund, so that fee (2.9% + 30¢ of the order for a Canadian card, more for an international one; for example about $1.61 on a $45 order) stays a cost to you. Refunds after work has started, and disputes or chargebacks, are handled in your own Stripe account.
