Support

Email racketsrestrung@gmail.com and we'll reply within one business day.

Common questions

Where is my order? Open the order from the home screen. The status updates as we receive, string and finish your rackets, and you'll get a notification when it's ready.

I didn't get my sign-in code. Codes arrive within a minute; check spam. You can also sign in with a password, or set one under Account.

How do I pay? By card or Apple Pay in the app when you place your order. Cancel before stringing starts and it is refunded in full.

How do I delete my account? Account screen, then Delete my account.
