Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Restrung is operated by ADHD Studios ("we", "us") in Kingston, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what the Restrung iOS app collects, why, and what you can do about it. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the name you enter, and a password if you set one. Used to sign you in and to label your rackets on our work tickets.
  • Orders: the rackets, strings, tensions and options you choose, when you placed the order, its status, and how you chose to pay. Used to do the work and to show you your history.
  • Address: only if you choose pickup or drop-off, and only for that order.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can tell you when your rackets are ready.
  • Club membership: the plan you request, its status, and the date it is paid through, if you join Restrung Club.
  • If you sign up as a stringer: your business name and type, the street address, city and province where rackets are dropped off, your hours, your prices, string menu and usual turnaround, and the plan you choose for paying Restrung. Once we approve your shop, its name, address, hours, prices, menu and turnaround are shown to customers choosing where to have a racket strung. You also see the orders placed at your shop, including the name each order was placed under.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. There are no ads and no analytics SDKs in the app.

Payments

Card and Apple Pay payments are handled by Stripe. You enter your card in Stripe's secure payment form; the card number goes directly to Stripe and is never seen or stored by the app or by us. We keep only a Stripe reference for each payment and, if you pay for Restrung Club by card, a reference to the saved card that Stripe uses for monthly renewals. If you choose a monthly stringer plan, Stripe also saves a card for that plan in the same way. Where Interac e-Transfer is offered, transfers go directly from your bank to us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. It is processed by service providers who act only on our instructions:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service. Data is stored on servers in the United States.
  • Resend (or our email provider) sends sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your device.
  • Stripe processes card and Apple Pay payments and refunds, and stores cards saved for Club renewals. Stripe may process data in the United States and other countries; see stripe.com/privacy.

We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account exists. Order records (what was ordered, prices, tax and payment status) are kept for bookkeeping for up to seven years, as Canadian tax rules require. Seven years after a deleted account's last order, the name on those records is removed as well.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen in the app. Deleting removes your login, email address, push token, notifications, the addresses on your orders and any Club membership immediately. If you applied as a stringer and your shop was never approved, the application is deleted too; an approved shop is closed to new orders and its order records are kept for bookkeeping. Past orders stay in our records for bookkeeping, including the name they were placed under, but can no longer be linked to a login. Payment records held by Stripe are kept by Stripe under its own legal obligations. You can also email us to request deletion.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing racketsrestrung@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Restrung is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. A parent or guardian should place orders on their behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
