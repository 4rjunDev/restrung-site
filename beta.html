Testing Restrung

Thanks for trying the app before it launches. Restrung is a real ordering app for racket stringing at Kingston Racquet Centre: the orders you place are real, the card payment is real, and your racket gets strung.

Getting the app

Install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the invitation link we sent you. TestFlight installs Restrung and updates it whenever we ship a new build.

Paying

You pay by card or Apple Pay in the app when you place the order, and the amount shown is what is charged. Orders cannot be cancelled from the app once placed; if something is wrong, contact the shop (its details are on the order) and they can cancel and refund it before stringing starts.

What to try

Create an account, order a restring, pay, then follow the order on its tracking screen. Before the stringer starts, try changing a racket's tension from the tracking screen. Save a racket so the next order takes a few taps. If you have questions about an order, the shop's details are on the order itself.

Telling us what you found

In TestFlight, open Restrung and use Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in the app and choose the feedback option that appears. Email racketsrestrung@gmail.com works too. Anything confusing is worth telling us, not only things that break.

Good to know

Beta builds expire 90 days after they are published; TestFlight will offer the next build before then. Your account and orders carry over to the App Store version when it launches.
